Overlay mode — feed a blank PS Form 3817

  1. Use Fill Fields to select a tenant — the To address fills automatically. Or click Edit in the toolbar to type it manually.
  2. Calibrate once: click Print, print on plain paper, hold it over a blank form to check that text lands on the lines. Nudge Up/Down and Left/Right above until aligned. Settings save automatically.
  3. Feed a blank PS Form 3817 into your printer's manual/bypass tray. Try face-up first; flip it if text lands on the wrong side.
  4. Click Print in the toolbar. Set paper size to 5″ × 3″ or select the manual feed tray.
  5. Hand to the postal worker — they stamp Postmark Here and return it as your proof of mailing.

Blank PS Form 3817 slips are free at any USPS post office counter — just ask at the window.

Full form — 3×5 index card

  1. Use Fill Fields to select a tenant, or click Edit to type the address manually.
  2. Load a 3×5 index card into your printer's manual/bypass tray.
  3. Click Print in the toolbar. In the print dialog, set paper size to 5″ × 3″ (custom size) or select the index card tray if your printer has one. Disable "fit to page" scaling.
  4. Hand the printed card to the postal worker — they stamp it and return it as your proof of mailing.

Prints the complete official form from scratch — no blank USPS slip needed.

Full form — Letter paper (top-left corner)

  1. Use Fill Fields to select a tenant, or click Edit to type the address manually.
  2. Load a standard 8.5″ × 11″ sheet into your printer.
  3. Click Print in the toolbar. Select Letter paper size. The form prints in the top-left corner; dashed lines mark where to cut.
  4. Cut along the dashed lines with scissors or a paper trimmer.
  5. Hand to the postal worker — they stamp it and return it as your proof of mailing.

Prints the complete official form from scratch — no blank USPS slip needed.
