What This Notice Means

This notice is a formal legal requirement when an owner decides to put a rental property on the market. Because you are the current resident, local law grants you the right of first refusal. This officially opens an exclusive window where you have the first option to negotiate the purchase of the property, should you be interested in buying it.

Important Reassurance for You

We understand that receiving a notice like this can cause uncertainty, so we want to be completely clear about your current living situation:

  • Nothing changes for you right now Your current lease agreement, rent terms, and tenancy remain fully in effect. This notice does not mean you have to move.
  • Continued property management We remain fully committed to managing and caring for the property just as we always have. Maintenance and day-to-day operations will continue without interruption throughout this process.
Showing Cooperation

As part of the sale process, a licensed real estate agent may need to schedule property showings. We kindly ask for your cooperation in allowing reasonable access for these visits, which will always be coordinated with advance notice in accordance with your lease. Your assistance is greatly appreciated and helps ensure a smooth process for everyone involved.
